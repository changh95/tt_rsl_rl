# TTNN issues, unsupported ops and workarounds

Per BRIEF 2-7: for every TTNN bug or unsupported op, a minimal repro, the observed vs expected behaviour, the tt-metal version, and the
documented workaround. Never patch the tt-metal tree. The repros are the device tests named below (each is a few lines of ttnn; run them
through `scripts/tt_run.sh`, see the docstring of each file).

Tested stack: PyPI wheel `ttnn==0.81.1` (same version as tt-metal `v0.81.1`, `d80d9482`), tt-umd `889651e`, sfpi 7.89.0, KMD 2.10.0, firmware bundle 19.13.1, Blackhole.

| T-15 | 2026-11-11 | Metal trace: intermediates are freed after the capture but rewritten by every replay | A tensor that is allocated AFTER a capture and keeps its value across replays can land on the addresses that the trace's intermediate tensors occupied, and the next `execute_trace` overwrites it. Observed (cartpole, act trace): the 8 tensors stored for rollout step 0 (allocated right after the capture, so at the lowest free addresses) changed between insertion and the end of the rollout; later steps were not hit. Computation inside the trace was correct (values right after each step identical to eager), so the symptom was a wrong surrogate loss (0.09 instead of 0.001) from iteration 0. The same hazard hit a second algorithm built after the first one's capture (std 1e6, NaN) and live tensors of one algorithm during the other's replays (GAE returns between `compute_returns` and `update`: 1024 of 1024 rows wrong). Nothing in ttnn warns about it | `scripts/probe_trace_diff.py` (insertion-time vs end-of-rollout snapshots), `tests/tt/test_tt_trace.py::test_traced_rollout_is_not_overwritten_by_later_replays`, `::test_a_second_algorithm_cannot_be_built_while_traces_are_alive` | PERSISTENT-STATE RULE: everything that survives a replay is allocated BEFORE the first capture (Adam moments, loss accumulators, storage constants, preallocated per-step rollout slots, `TTRolloutStorage(persistent=True)`); buffers that are rewritten before every use are exempt; nothing that is read after a replay may be created before it in the same call (`update()` builds the normalizer inputs after the epochs); one training algorithm per process, never interleaved with another one's replays; `TTPPO.__init__` refuses to build while a captured trace is alive on the mesh | worked around (by design of the traced path) |
